MA3 LOCAL BUSINESS POSTERS: 100 demo businesses

MASTER STYLE (paste once at the top of your Stitch / image AI chat, then give the lines below one by one)
Create a landscape picture, 3:2, 1200x800. Bright, warm, friendly Nairobi street feel. Show the business place or its main goods clearly in the middle. Add the business name as large clean text across the top. Show the services as 3-5 small icons with short labels along the bottom. Colours: warm orange and deep navy with white. Do NOT include phone numbers, prices, real logos or real people's faces. Do not copy any brand. One picture per business.

HOW TO USE: for each line make the picture, save it with the FILE name, upload all files to Supabase Storage > business-images > demo/ , then run supabase/seed/demo_local_businesses.sql.
